"If you leave unsatisfied, you don't pay."

Unsatisfied means you leave your initial physiotherapy appointment without one or more of: a diagnosis, a written plan, or a strategy for getting back to training.

If that happens, contact us by phone or email within seven days of the appointment and the initial fee is refunded in full.

This commitment covers the appointment itself. It does not cover pain relief, recovery speed, or a result by a particular date. No clinician can guarantee those and we don't.

It applies to private-paying initial appointments only. Where a private health claim has been processed, the claim is reversed before the refund is issued. Follow-up appointments are outside this commitment.

Requests made through this page are appointment requests, not confirmed bookings. A booking is confirmed when you accept a time we send you. We ask for 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment.

Nothing here affects your rights under Australian Consumer Law.
