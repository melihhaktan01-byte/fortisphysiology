Why it keeps coming back

Pain-free walking doesn't mean you're ready to roll.

Most grapplers try the same things with an injury. Rest until the pain goes, a brace, lighter partners, skipping takedowns, a sheet of generic exercises, then straight back into live rounds. The pain settles, so it feels fixed. Then the first hard roll loads it in a way nothing at home did, and it goes again.

The step everyone skips is the one between pain-free and mat-ready: finding out what the sport is asking of that knee or shoulder, and building back to it on purpose. That's what the check is for.

Frequently asked questions

How does the discount voucher work?

The check is the kind of assessment other Sydney clinics charge up to $80 for. With the voucher you pay $49 at booking. If you go ahead with the Return-to-Mat Work-Up within 7 days of your check, the full $49 comes off its price. So if you continue, the check cost you nothing.

Can I keep training?

That's what the check answers. Your physio trains, so the written plan covers what to modify on the mats and what to keep. Sometimes the answer is "keep going with changes". Sometimes it isn't. You'll know why either way.

What's the Return-to-Mat Work-Up?

The full first appointment: 60 minutes one-on-one, a complete movement and strength work-up, diagnosis of the root cause, treatment starting the same day where appropriate, your written plan, and your physio's direct contact. $180, with your $49 credited.

Is this only for grapplers?

No. It's the same process for the gym, other sports, work injuries and everyday pain. Grapplers are who we see most. It's not for emergencies, NDIS-funded care or telehealth.

Can I claim on private health?

Yes, for the Work-Up and any treatment after it, if your extras cover includes physiotherapy. HICAPS on the spot, you pay the gap. No referral needed.

Can I use a Medicare care plan, WorkCover, CTP or DVA?

Yes, for treatment. If you've had the injury for 6 months or more, ask your GP about a chronic condition management plan. For WorkCover or a car accident claim, bring your claim number. Call or text 0424 309 389 before you book so we can check your claim has been accepted.

What if I need to cancel?

Text as early as you can and we'll move you to another time, your $49 moves with you. 24 hours' notice is required.

Where are you, and what should I wear?

10 Raglan Road, Auburn, inside the Sydney Wrestling Academy building, plenty of street parking. Wear clothes you can move in. Bring any scans or reports.
