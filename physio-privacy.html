Privacy

How we handle your information

Fortis Physiology (10 Raglan Road, Auburn NSW 2144) collects personal information so we can book, treat and follow up with you. We handle it under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect. Your name, contact details, date of birth, health fund details, and the health information you share with your therapist. Online bookings are made through Cliniko, which stores booking and clinical records on our behalf in Australia.

Why. To provide physiotherapy and exercise physiology services, process health fund claims, send appointment confirmations and reminders, and contact you about your care. We do not sell your information.

Advertising and analytics. This page uses the Meta Pixel and Microsoft Clarity. They record page visits and button clicks so we can measure our ads and improve the page. They do not receive your health information. You can limit this in your browser settings and in your Meta ad preferences.

Who sees it. Your treating practitioners, and only with your consent your GP, specialist, insurer or health fund. Clinical records are kept for at least seven years as required in NSW.

Your rights. You can ask to see or correct your information, or make a complaint, by emailing contact@fortisphysiology.com. If you're not satisfied with our response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
