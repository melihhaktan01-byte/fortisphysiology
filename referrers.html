What to refer

If exercise or rehab is indicated, we take it.

Musculoskeletal injuries

Back, neck, shoulder, hip and knee, acute or persistent.

Sports & training injuries

Assessment, rehab and staged return to sport.

Work-related injuries

Active claims, with return-to-work planning and reporting.

Persistent pain & deconditioning

Patients needing structured, supervised exercise rather than passive care.

Chronic conditions

Type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular and metabolic conditions where exercise is indicated.

Older adults

Falls risk, balance, mobility and independence, dosed safely.

How it works, start to finish

  1. You send the referral, or the patient contacts us directly
  2. We contact the patient and arrange the first appointment
  3. Structured initial assessment with the treating clinician
  4. You receive an assessment letter with the diagnosis and plan
  5. Active block of care, then a discharge summary back to you

What you can expect from us

  • Capacity for new patients, onboarded at the earliest available appointment
  • Correspondence after the initial assessment
  • Progress updates on request, and at discharge
  • Evidence-based, active care, exercise is the core of treatment
  • Medicare, DVA, workers compensation, CTP, My Aged Care, private extras
