04 / For Referrers

Referring a patient
is simple.

We accept referrals from GPs, specialists, and allied health for both exercise physiology and physiotherapy. Every referred patient gets a structured assessment, a clear plan, and correspondence back to you.

We accept
  • Medicare Chronic Disease Management plansExercise physiology and physiotherapy under a GP-managed CDM plan
  • Workers compensation (NSW)Structured, documented rehab with return-to-work focus
  • Private health extrasClaimable where the patient's cover includes EP or physio
  • Private patients, no referral neededPatients can self-refer for both services
How to refer

Email the referral and any relevant history to contact@fortisphysiology.com, or have the patient contact us directly. We will report back after the initial assessment.
