What We Do

From first consult to full capacity.

01

Injury Assessment & Rehab

Physiotherapy assessment, diagnosis and a staged rehab plan for back, neck, shoulder, hip and knee injuries, with clear milestones so you know you are progressing.

02

Strength & Conditioning

Individualised programming from an accredited exercise physiologist: technique, load management and progression, including athletes and combat sports.

03

Return to Sport

Criteria-based progressions that close the gap between "cleared" and genuinely ready: strength benchmarks, capacity testing, then sport-specific loading.

04

Chronic Disease Management

Supervised clinical exercise for cardiovascular, metabolic and musculoskeletal conditions, dosed to the person and their comorbidities.

05

Type 2 Diabetes Management

Structured exercise to support glycaemic control, strength and daily function, coordinated with the patient's GP care plan.

06

Work Injury Rehabilitation

Documented, goal-based rehab for injured workers with progress reporting and a clear return-to-work focus.

For Referrers

Referring a patient is simple.

We accept referrals from GPs, specialists and allied health for exercise physiology and physiotherapy. Every referred patient receives a structured initial assessment and you receive correspondence after that assessment, then at discharge.

What to refer
  • Musculoskeletal injuriesBack, neck, shoulder, hip and knee, acute or persistent
  • Sports & training injuriesAssessment, rehab and staged return to sport
  • Work-related injuriesActive claims, with return-to-work planning and reporting
  • Persistent pain & deconditioningPatients needing structured, supervised exercise rather than passive care
  • Chronic conditions where exercise is indicatedIncluding type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular and metabolic conditions
Funding we accept
  • Medicare Chronic Disease Management plansEP and physiotherapy under a GP-managed CDM plan
  • Workers compensation (NSW)Documented rehab with return-to-work focus
  • Private health extrasWhere the patient's cover includes EP or physio
  • Private patientsNo referral needed, patients can self-refer

How it works, start to finish

  1. You send the referral, or the patient contacts us directly
  2. We contact the patient and arrange the first appointment
  3. Structured initial assessment with the treating clinician
  4. You receive an assessment letter with the diagnosis and plan
  5. Active block of care, then a discharge summary back to you

To refer, email us with:

  • Patient name, DOB and contact details
  • Presenting condition and relevant history
  • Any imaging or specialist reports
  • CDM plan paperwork, if applicable
  • Claim number and insurer details, for workers compensation
