What We Do

01

Injury Assessment & Rehab

Common Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need a referral?

No, private patients can book directly. You only need paperwork for funded care: a GP care plan for Medicare rebates, or insurer approval for a workers compensation or CTP claim.

Can I just walk in?

No, we are appointment only. Call or text 0434 068 216 with your name and what is going on, and we will reply with available times.

Physiotherapist or exercise physiologist, which one do I need?

A physiotherapist assesses, diagnoses and treats pain and injury. An exercise physiologist prescribes structured exercise to rebuild capacity, for rehab, chronic conditions and performance. Many people move from one to the other as they progress. If you are not sure, contact us, we will point you to the right clinician after hearing what is going on.

What will it cost?

We confirm all fees with you before your first appointment, no surprises. Rebates may apply through a Medicare care plan, private health extras, DVA, CTP, or workers compensation. We have HICAPS, so extras claims happen on the spot.

I have a workers compensation claim. Can you help?

Yes. We provide structured, documented rehab for injured workers in Auburn and Western Sydney under NSW workers compensation, with progress reporting and a clear return-to-work focus.

Do you speak Turkish?

Yes. Consultations are available in English and Turkish, evet, Türkçe konuşuyoruz.

Do you only work with athletes?

No. We work with everyday people in pain, injured workers, and people managing chronic conditions. The same standards apply whether your goal is a comp podium or carrying the groceries without pain.

Is exercise safe for my condition?

Exercise is prescribed like a dose: matched to your condition, your history and your capacity, then progressed under supervision. That is exactly what an accredited exercise physiologist is trained to do.
