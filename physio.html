What happens in your treatment

So you know exactly what you're booking.

PHOTO: the treatment room, wide, or a mid-session shot
The conversation

Your history, what's going on right now, what your training week looks like, your goals in the sport, and what a good outcome looks like to you.

The assessment

A detailed look at how you move, where you're strong and where you're not, and the positions that set it off. Explained in plain language, not physio-speak.

Treatment

Hands-on treatment where it's appropriate, in the same appointment, plus the first exercises you'll actually do.

The plan

What to modify on the mats, what to keep, what happens next, and your physio's direct contact for anything in between.

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep training?

That's what the assessment answers. Your physio trains, so the plan covers what to modify on the mats and what to keep. Sometimes the answer is "keep going with changes". Sometimes it isn't. You'll know why either way.

Is this only for grapplers?

No. It's the same process for the gym, other sports, work injuries and everyday pain. Grapplers are who we see most. It's not for emergencies, NDIS-funded care or telehealth.

How many appointments will I need?

Depends on the injury and the goal. Your physio tells you what to expect after the assessment. We don't sell packages.

Can I claim on private health?

Yes, if your extras cover includes physiotherapy. HICAPS on the spot, you pay the gap. No referral needed.

What does "direct access outside clinic hours" mean?

Once you're a patient you have your therapist's personal contact details. Message them with questions or a flare-up, they reply when free, usually the same day, and adjust the plan if needed. Not for emergencies.

Where are you, and what should I wear?

10 Raglan Road, Auburn, inside the Sydney Wrestling Academy building, plenty of street parking. Wear clothes you can move in. Bring any scans or reports and your health fund card.

What if I need to cancel?

Text as early as you can and we'll move you. 24 hours' notice is required, otherwise a cancellation fee applies.
